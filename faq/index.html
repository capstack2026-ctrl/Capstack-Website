Process & Collaboration

A typical engagement moves through understanding the requirements, defining the scope and architecture, building and integrating, testing and launch preparation, then planning useful improvements.

We start with the business goals, the users, the current workflow and the constraints. That context helps turn a broad need into a workable project scope.

Project decisions need client context. The level and rhythm of collaboration are agreed during planning so feedback and approvals have a clear place in the work.

Changes are best considered against the agreed scope, technical impact and priorities. We discuss what needs to change before treating it as part of the project plan.

Testing is planned around realistic scenarios and the requirements of the project. The exact checks depend on what is being built, the people using it and the systems it connects to.

Pricing & Engagement

Cost depends on the scope, complexity, integrations and the work required. We discuss the requirement first, then provide a scoped proposal for the project.

Engagement and quoting arrangements depend on the project. The suitable structure is discussed once the work and its requirements are understood.

Often, a focused first piece of work is the best place to begin. We can help identify the smallest useful website, workflow, integration or product version to address the priority.

Scope, technical complexity, third-party systems, decisions and feedback all affect timing. A realistic schedule is considered during project planning rather than assumed in advance.

Support & Maintenance

Post-launch needs vary by project. Support, maintenance and improvement requirements can be discussed as part of planning; the exact scope is agreed for the engagement.
