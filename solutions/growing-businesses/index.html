12 / FAQ

Practical questions, answered clearly.

How do we know whether custom software is the right answer?

We begin with the business problem and the current process. Sometimes software is the right answer; sometimes a better workflow, website improvement or integration is more useful. We will help you assess the options.

Can you improve a system we already use?

Often, yes. We can look at where the current system creates friction, then recommend whether to improve, connect or replace the relevant parts.

What can business process automation handle?

Automation can help with repeatable, rule-based work such as capturing enquiries, routing information, syncing data, sending notifications and updating connected tools.

Can you connect the tools our team already uses?

Where the systems provide suitable ways to connect, we can map and build integrations that reduce duplicate data entry and disconnected workflows.

Do we need to know exactly what to build before talking?

No. A useful starting point is the bottleneck you want to remove, the people it affects and the outcome you need. We can help turn that into a practical scope.

How is a project scoped?

We discuss the goals, current setup, constraints and priorities, then define the most useful first piece of work. Project cost and timing are confirmed in a scoped proposal.
