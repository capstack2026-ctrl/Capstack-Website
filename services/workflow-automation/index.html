01 / The business problem

Manual work is a cost you pay every day

Every business has them: the form that someone retypes into a spreadsheet, the lead that waits in an inbox, the report assembled by hand every Friday, the invoice that depends on someone remembering.

Individually these tasks look small. Together they eat hours, introduce mistakes and slow down everything that depends on them. As the business grows, the problem grows with it.

Automation removes the repetition. But automation built carelessly creates its own problems: workflows that break silently, data that goes to the wrong place, and processes nobody understands once the person who built them has left.

We build automation the way we build software: mapped first, engineered properly, tested, and documented.

05 / How we work

A clear process from first conversation to go-live

  1. 01

    Understand.

    We learn how the process works today: who does what, which tools are involved, where time is lost and where errors happen.

  2. 02

  3. 03

  4. 04

  5. 05

    We go live with you, monitor early runs closely and make adjustments.

  6. 06

    After launch we remain available for fixes and refinements, and to automate the next process when you are ready.

Duration depends on the complexity of the workflow and the tools involved, and is confirmed in your proposal.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What is workflow automation?

Workflow automation uses software to carry out repetitive steps in a business process automatically, such as moving data between tools, sending notifications or generating reports, so people don't have to do them by hand.

What kinds of tasks can be automated?

Anything that follows clear rules and repeats: capturing and routing enquiries, syncing data between tools, sending reminders, producing reports, and moving a client through a standard onboarding sequence. In your first conversation we help identify where automation would pay off.

How much does automation cost?

It depends on the number of workflows, the tools involved and the complexity of the logic. We quote each project individually after mapping your process.

How long does it take?

A simple workflow can be much quicker than a multi-step process spanning several tools. We confirm a realistic schedule in your proposal.

What if an automation breaks?

Workflows are built with error handling and alerts, so problems are flagged instead of ignored.

Is my data safe?

Security is part of how we build, including careful handling of credentials and limiting what each workflow can access. No system can be promised risk-free, but we apply sound practices and test before go-live.

Do I need to change the tools I already use?

Usually not. We aim to connect the tools you already have. If a tool genuinely blocks a good workflow, we will tell you and discuss options.

Do you work with clients outside India?

Yes. We are based in Chennai and work with clients across India and worldwide, collaborating remotely.

Can you also build the software or website my automation connects to?

Yes. See Website Development and SaaS & Product Development.
