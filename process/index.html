PROCESS FAQ

A clearer way to start.

How does a project with Capstack begin?

We begin with the business problem, people involved, current setup and desired outcome. That conversation helps establish whether there is a practical fit and what to explore next.

What information should we prepare before starting?

Helpful context includes your goals, users, existing systems, constraints and examples of what is not working today. You do not need a complete technical specification to begin.

How involved will we be during development?

Your business context is important throughout the project. The review and approval points are planned around the scope so decisions can be made with the right information.

Can project requirements change during development?

They can be assessed as the project develops. We consider the impact on scope, priorities and the technical approach before changes are incorporated into the plan.

How long does a typical development project take?

Timing depends on the scope, complexity, integrations and decision-making needed. A realistic project schedule is considered after the work is understood and scoped.

What happens after the project is launched?

Launch preparation includes verification of the agreed solution. Any handover, documentation, support or future improvement needs are discussed in the context of the engagement.
